Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Purpose and scope

Essential storage supports basic site preferences. Optional analytics is disabled by default and activates only after consent. You can reject optional cookies through the consent notice and clear stored preferences in your browser.

This policy applies to the public pages, contact form, journal, collection index and consent controls of Gentle Sock Archive. It does not govern third-party websites that may be linked for reference.

Information and website use

Information may be supplied directly through an enquiry or created automatically through ordinary technical requests required to display a page. We limit collection to what is reasonably necessary for communication, security, accessibility and basic site operation.

Analytics and consent

Optional analytics remains denied until a visitor selects Accept in the privacy notice. Visitors can reject optional analytics without losing access to editorial content. Browser settings may also be used to remove previously stored preferences.

Sharing, retention and safeguards

We do not sell personal information. Limited data may be handled by service providers that support hosting, security or communications, subject to appropriate instructions. Enquiries are retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, maintain records or meet legal obligations.

Intellectual property and acceptable use

Original text, visual presentation and archive organisation are protected by applicable intellectual-property rules. Personal reading and ordinary linking are welcome. Automated extraction, misleading republication, interference with the site or use that violates another person’s rights is prohibited.

Editorial limitations

sock names and collection entries are illustrative editorial references. Material, sizing, care and performance information is general and may not suit every product or wearer. Confirm important decisions with the manufacturer, retailer or an appropriately qualified specialist.

Your choices and rights

Depending on location, visitors may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of eligible personal information. Requests can be sent to editorial@gentlesockarchive.com. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Changes to this page

We may revise this information when site functions, legal requirements or editorial practices change. The updated date above identifies the latest version. Continued use after a revision means the current terms and notices apply.
